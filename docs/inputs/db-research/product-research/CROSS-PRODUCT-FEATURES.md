# Cross-product feature discovery

8 October 2026. Comparisons are bounded to the pinned sources and historical results linked below. “Better” means a mechanism better fits a named requirement; no new usability/performance benchmark was run. Native product functionality, supported external APIs and our proposed integration remain distinct.

## Proposed enhancement value

The catalog has **45 ENHANCEMENT** and **32 DIFFERENTIATOR** items, plus 112 BASELINE, 24 OPTIONAL and 7 REJECTED. The 77 enhancement/differentiator records are useful proposed capabilities, not 77 verified features absent from Bytebase. Several are stronger semantics than a partial existing capability. Source product evidence and design status are recorded independently for every item.

## Deliberate exclusions

Do not blend every portal and engine into one installation. There is one approval authority and one executor per stream. ODC/Archery + external engine integrations are not native upstream migration features; no direct metadata edits or fabricated ticket SUCCESS callbacks are recommended. A full product fork has patch/security/license cost even when its source grant is permissive.

Reject unrestricted provisioning, automatic arbitrary Oracle DDL rollback, autonomous AI approval/recovery, unreviewed production MCP writes, vendor branding and commercial quotas. Defer broad bulk data tools, advanced observability requiring Oracle packs, EBR, visual query building and proprietary Oracle desired-state adapters. Useful features must earn their scope through an operating need and acceptance test.
