Release-blocking risks are R-01–09, R-11–13, R-17 and R-19/20 until their applicable acceptance evidence exists. Operational budgets do not lower correctness gates. Risks involving unavailable lab conditions stay NOT_TESTED; a source reference or historical product run cannot close them.

The highest uncertainty is the parser/driver/lock combination, followed by crash consistency, approval binding and Oracle validity visibility. Those define the first implementation milestone. The strict OSS goal is achievable in principle with the Thin driver candidate, but the new migration coordinator remains substantial unproven software and must be estimated/maintained accordingly.
