## Evidence files and levels

[Source references](source-references.json) hold exact repository, full SHA, path, line interval, content SHA-256, URL, claim and provenance. [Document references](document-references.json) hold primary URL, date and bounded summary. [Surface inventory](bytebase-surface-inventory.json) enumerates API/route/model/edition declarations. [Upstream observations](upstream-observations.json) and [snapshot register](snapshot-register.json) distinguish current heads from historical source.

SOURCE_VERIFIED: inspected source mechanism/contract at a pin. DOC_VERIFIED: observed official/repository documentation, including explicitly limited index-only facts. RUNTIME_VERIFIED: historical observed behavior on its exact recorded build only. DESIGN_PROPOSAL: intended new behavior. UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence. NOT_TESTED: no runtime verification for the new platform. Source evidence never establishes complete licensing rights or new runtime success.

Documentation URLs are live and can change. No full commercial/license agreement is certified by a website summary. See [limitations](research-limitations.md).
